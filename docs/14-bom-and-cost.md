Generated by `tools/cost_rollup.py` from `hardware/bom/bom.csv`.

**These are planning estimates, not quotes.** Unit costs were entered from general knowledge of 2026 pricing and have not been checked line by line against live distributor stock. Two were checked against published list prices on 2026-11-10: the Compute Module 5 family (the maker's July 2026 product brief) and the 5 inch Touch Display 2 ($40 list).
