## Runtime

Usable capacity is taken as 90% of rated. Target: 8 h typical.

| Profile | Avg W | 1S2P (36 Wh) | 1S3P (54 Wh) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Typical field day (35 % testing, 65 % idle/dimmed) | 4.85 | 6.7 h | 10.0 h |
| Heavy (70 % testing) | 6.31 | 5.1 h | 7.7 h |
| Continuous testing | 7.56 | 4.3 h | 6.4 h |

## Reading

- A two-cell pack gives about 6.7 h on the typical profile: short of the 8 h target.
- A three-cell pack gives about 10.0 h: meets the target with +2.0 h margin. **Rev A is designed around 1S3P.**
- Continuous testing does not reach 8 h on either pack. The 8 h claim is for the typical profile only and must not be advertised more broadly until measured.
- Standby (SoM off, coprocessor alive, ~4 mW estimate): about 506 days. Cold boot from standby is a full Linux boot (target under 20 s, unmeasured); the SoM has no suspend-to-RAM we can rely on.
- Peak: the SoM is specified for up to 5 A at 5 V. At 3.3 V battery that is about 8.4 A from the pack, at the edge of the boost converter. The CPU cap on battery is what keeps peaks inside the budget; see risk R-05.
- PoE load tests dissipate the PoE's power, not the battery's.

## What Phase 7 must measure

1. SoM current at idle, during a scan, and during an iperf3 run, with the CPU cap applied.
2. Backlight current against brightness.
3. Boost efficiency at 3.3 V, 3.7 V and 4.1 V input.
4. Full discharge runtime on the typical profile script (`docs/09`, test PWR-04).
